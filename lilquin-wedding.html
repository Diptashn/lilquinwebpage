What we build

Every wedding cake is sketched and bite-tested before a single gram of flour goes in. We start with your palette — florist colours, venue tones, your vision — and end with a cake that disappears as quickly as it appears.

  • Flavours — vanilla & brown butter; pistachio & raspberry; Valrhona chocolate; lemon & elderflower; salted oaked pear; rum-soaked raisin; violet & rose; spiced pear & ginger; salted honeycomb; ube mascarpone.
  • Sizes — 2 tiers (Tier B 16"x7" + Tier C 21"x36"). 3 tiers (add Tier A 26"x46"). Hand-coded cake charts with preferred serve count per tier.
  • Finish — silk buttercream; fresh florals; hand-painted; gold leaf; piped beaded borders.

What’s included

Sketch consult · florals · delivery · on-site assembly · touch-ups · hand-coded cake chart with boxed serve counts per tier & weight per tier.

How it works

Choose a date

Suggest your month and weekend. We’ll email back with availability and a short questionnaire about numbers, flavours, and florist colours.

Book a tasting

Choose Saturday 10–5 in Soho (tastings £35, taken off cake). Taste six signature flavours and bring your sketch if you’ve started talking with a florist.

We sketch and sign off

We draw a dryer with nob on it (your cake) and confirm details: tiers, heights, serve count, flavour per layer, chantilly, calendar, quote.

We bake and deliver

We bake your cake the week of. On the morning of, we arrive to stack and decorate. Two of us usually. Two hours is usually enough.

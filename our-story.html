Our Story — Est. 2014, Bekasi

LilQuin Kitchen: Hand‑crafted Cakes That Taste as Beautiful as They Look.

Born in a modest home kitchen in Bekasi, LilQuin Kitchen began with a single hand mixer, a treasured family‑recipe notebook, and a simple conviction: every cake should deliver authentic flavor as well as visual delight.

What started as birthday treats for friends quickly grew into word‑of‑mouth praise, culminating into a wedding‑cake request “Bake a cake that tastes authentic, not just decorative.” That became our brief for everything since: prioritizing taste while still maintaining aesthetic.

Today we operate from a purpose‑built studio—converted from a garage featuring a dedicated oven and a sun‑lit workspace that lets us focus on 3 wedding cakes per week. This intentional pace ensures each creation receives undivided attention, from the initial idea to the final, morning‑of stack. Our commitment to quality shows in every detail.

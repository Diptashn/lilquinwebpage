Our Story — Est. 2014, Bekasi

A small kitchen with one rule: flavour first.

LilQuin Kitchen started in a home kitchen in Soho with a secondhand mixer, a notebook of family recipes, and a belief that a cake should taste as good as it looks.

It began with birthday cakes for friends. Then friends of friends. Then a wedding cake for a neighbour who said, “just make it taste like something real.” That became our brief for everything since: small-batch sponges, Swiss meringue buttercream, fresh florals, nothing frozen, nothing rushed.

By 2021 we had our garage renovated into a tiny studio with one oven and a big window. We made a decision early that still shapes how we work: we bake one wedding cake a day. No parallel tiers, no catalogue repeats. Your cake gets the whole kitchen, from sketch to morning-of stacking.

How we work today

Every order starts the same way — a conversation and a tasting. Six signature cubes to take home, a sketch drawn around your florist’s palette, and an honest quote. For birthdays, that means 48-hour icons you can actually order in time. For weddings, it means delivery, on-site assembly, and touch-ups while you get ready.

We are still small on purpose. The same team that answers your email pipes your buttercream. If something isn’t right, we fix it. If a flavour isn’t in season, we’ll tell you what is better.

— Yusi, founder & head baker

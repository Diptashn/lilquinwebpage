Fully custom, down to the last sprinkle

Every birthday design is fully custom to your desires — flavour, size, colours, message, toppers. Send a sketch, a photo, or just an idea and we bake it into a one-of-a-kind cake for your table. No catalogue copies, no shortcuts.

  • Buttercream — silk Swiss meringue, hand-piped textures and messages.
  • Whipped cream — lighter finish, best with fresh fruit and soft palettes.
  • Fondant icing — crisp, sculpted, for bold themes and figurines.
  • Sizes — 6" (10 slices) · 8" (20 slices) · two-tier party cakes on request.

How it works

01 — Tell us the idea. Date, serves, flavours, and a photo or theme you love.

02 — We sketch and confirm. Quote, design sign-off, and bake slot within 48 hours.

03 — We bake the week of. Never frozen. Pick up or two-person delivery with touch-ups.

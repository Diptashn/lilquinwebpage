Your idea, realised as art

We want to fully embrace your idea and design and realise it into a one of a kind art for your special occasion. Share a sketch, a fabric swatch, a floral palette or a wild concept — we develop the proportions, flavours and finish together and then bake it into a cake that could only be yours.

  • Elegant — clean lines, silk buttercream, fresh florals, gold leaf.
  • Extravagant — stacked tiers, textured piping, sculpted sugar, hand-painted layers.
  • Finish — buttercream · whipped cream · fondant — matched to the design, never the other way around.
  • Service — sketch consult, flavour tasting, delivery and on-site assembly.

How we work

01 — You brief the dream. Mood, palette, venue, guests — as loose or precise as you like.

02 — We design. A sketched proposal with tiers, flavours and finish for sign-off.

03 — We bake and dress. Baked the week of, finished morning-of, styled at the venue.
